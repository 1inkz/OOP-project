A. GAME BASICS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What is The Sims Console Edition?
A: It's a text-based life simulation game where you create and manage virtual 
   characters (Sims), control their daily lives, manage their finances, and 
   help them achieve their goals.

Q: Can I play with multiple characters?
A: Yes! You can create multiple Sims and manage them individually. However, 
   only one Sim is active at a time. You switch between them from the Sims 
   Management Menu.

Q: How do I switch between Sims?
A: From the Sims Management Menu, select "Select Existing Sims" to choose a 
   different character to control. Each Sim can be managed independently.

Q: How long is a typical game session?
A: A single day takes roughly 14 in-game hours (08:00 to 22:00), which 
   translates to about 15-30 minutes of actual gameplay depending on your pace.

Q: What's the goal of the game?
A: There's no single win condition. The game is open-ended - you set your own 
   goals: build wealth, advance careers, create a dream home, etc. You lose 
   if your Sim dies (needs reach zero).

Q: How do I win?
A: You set your own win conditions! Common user goals include:
   • Accumulate $50,000+ in wealth
   • Reach maximum job skill level (10)
   • Own multiple properties
   • Support multiple Sims
   • Pay off all debts

Q: Is there a story mode?
A: No, the game is sandbox/simulation-based. You create your own story by 
   making decisions and managing your Sims' lives.

Q: Can I customize my Sim's appearance?
A: The console version focuses on gameplay mechanics rather than visual 
   customization. Appearance is text-based (age type: Child/Adult/Elder).

B. NEEDS & HEALTH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What are needs?
A: Needs are basic requirements your Sim must maintain to stay alive:
   • Hunger - Need to eat regularly
   • Energy - Need to rest and sleep
   • Hygiene - Need to shower and stay clean
   • Bladder - Need to use the toilet
   • Mood - Need entertainment and comfort

Q: What happens if a need reaches zero?
A: Your Sim DIES instantly. This is game over! Always monitor your needs, 
   especially the red (≤20) status ones.

Q: How do I manage needs?
A: Each need has associated actions at specific locations:
   • Hunger → Eat at Restaurant
   • Energy → Sleep at Home
   • Hygiene → Shower at Home
   • Bladder → Use Toilet at Home
   • Mood → Play at Park or socialize

Q: How often do needs increase?
A: Needs change every in-game hour. Each action your Sim takes consumes 1 hour 
   of time and affects needs differently.

Q: Can I check my needs without viewing full status?
A: Currently, you must view full status to see exact need levels. The location 
   action menu shows needs but not specific values.

Q: What does the need color mean?
A: 
   • GREEN (≥60) - Excellent, no immediate action needed
   • YELLOW (21-59) - Warning, address soon
   • RED (≤20) - Critical, needs immediate attention!

Q: What's the sleep schedule?
A: 
   • 20:00 - Sleep reminder message
   • 21:00 - Sleep warning message
   • 22:00 - Forced faint (Sim collapses to sleep, advances to next day)

Q: Can I sleep during the day?
A: No, sleep only happens at night at home. Your Sim will be forced to sleep 
   at 22:00 regardless of location or activity.

Q: What happens after sleep?
A: Your Sim advances to the next day and all Sims in your household also move 
   to the next day. This synchronizes the game world.

Q: Do needs change while I'm not actively playing?
A: No. Needs only change when you perform actions (which consume time). Idle 
   time doesn't affect needs.

Q: Can I die from something other than needs?
A: Yes, two other death conditions:
   1. Loan default: After day 80, if (Bank + Simcoin) < Loan, Sim dies
   2. Repossession: Assets can be taken if loan is too high after day 60

C. JOBS & CAREER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: How do I get a job?
A: From the Main Menu, select "Find Job / Change Job" and choose from available 
   positions. You're hired immediately.

Q: What jobs are available?
A:
   1. Chef - Work at Restaurant
   2. Doctor - Work at Hospital
   3. Engineer - Technical work
   4. Influencer - Social media work
   5. Jobless (default job)- No income, no work

Q: Can I have multiple jobs?
A: You can only have one job at a time. Switching jobs replaces your 
   previous job immediately.

Q: Is there a penalty for switching jobs?
A: You lose your skill level (resets to 1) but can switch freely without 
   financial penalty.

Q: How much money do I earn per job?
A: Job pay varies by job type and skill level:
   • Base salary ranges from $200-$800 per work session
   • Skill levels 1-10 increase pay incrementally
   • Higher skill = higher income

Q: How do I advance my job skill?
A: Work repeatedly at your job. Each work session at the job location increases 
   your skill level by small increments. Progression is gradual (weeks of work).

Q: What's the maximum job skill?
A: Level 10 is the maximum. Once you reach it, you've achieved full mastery and 
   earn the maximum salary for that job.

Q: Can I have a job without going to a specific location?
A: Most jobs require location-based work. Some jobs may have different mechanics, 
   so check job descriptions at their locations.

Q: What does "Jobless" do?
A: Jobless provides no income but also has no job responsibilities. You're free 
   to pursue other goals without job time commitments.

Q: How is job income different from other income sources?
A: Job income is the primary steady income source. It's reliable and increases 
   with skill. Other income sources may exist but are limited.

Q: Can a child Sim have the same jobs as an adult?
A: Yes, but Child Sims may have different income scaling or restrictions. Check 
   the game for age-specific job differences.

Q: Do I need specific skills to get a job?
A: No prerequisites. You can get any job immediately. However, some jobs may be 
   unavailable at certain life stages (child vs. adult vs. elder).

D. MONEY & BANKING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Where do I get money?
A: Primary sources:
   1. Starting money (given at game start)
   2. Job income (earned per work session)
   3. Selling assets (return partial value)
   4. Finding money (if applicable)

Q: What's the difference between Simcoin and Bank Account?
A:
   • Simcoin = Cash on hand (visible, easy to spend)
   • Bank Account = Savings (stored safely, earns interest potentially)

Q: How do I deposit money?
A: Visit the Bank → Select "Deposit" → Enter amount. Money moves from Simcoin 
   to Bank Account.

Q: How do I withdraw money?
A: Visit the Bank → Select "Withdraw" → Enter amount. Money moves from Bank 
   Account to Simcoin.

Q: Do I lose money over time?
A: No, your money stays safe unless you spend it or pay loans. No automatic 
   deductions exist.

Q: Can I go negative on money?
A: No. If you don't have enough Simcoin, you can't perform that purchase or 
   action. You could take a loan instead.

Q: What's a loan?
A: A loan is borrowed money from the game's bank. You must repay it with 
   interest. Used primarily for down payments on major purchases.

Q: How much can I borrow?
A: Maximum $5000 total loan amount. You can't exceed this limit.

Q: How do I repay a loan?
A: Visit the Bank → Select "Repay Loan" → Enter amount to repay. Money deducted 
   from Simcoin.

Q: What happens if I don't repay my loan?
A: After day 80:
   • If (Bank Balance + Simcoin) < Loan Amount = Your Sim DIES (Game Over)
   This is a fail condition, so manage loans carefully!

Q: Can assets be taken if I owe money?
A: Yes. After day 60, if your loan is unmanageable:
   • If loan > $2000: Lose House (if owned), Loan cleared, but Car may remain
   • If loan < $2000: Lose Car, Loan cleared
   • Repossession is automatic - no warning!

Q: Are there hidden costs?
A: Yes - each location action costs time (1 hour), which affects needs:
   • Travel: -15 energy (walking), -5 energy (driving), +10 hunger (walking)
   • Work: Varies by job type
   • Eating: Reduces hunger but costs time
   
   Plan your budget carefully!

Q: What's a good starting financial strategy?
A: 
   1. Get a job immediately (establish income)
   2. Work 2-3 times to build capital
   3. Save $1500 for house down payment
   4. Buy house ASAP (essential for sleep)
   5. Then save for car ($400 down payment)
   6. Build emergency fund in bank

E. ASSETS & PROPERTY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What are assets?
A: Major purchases that provide benefits and represent wealth:
   • Car - Faster travel, reduced energy cost
   • House - Place to sleep, shower, rest

Q: How much does a car cost?
A: $2000 full price or $400 down payment (with $1600 loan).

Q: How much does a house cost?
A: $5000 full price or $1500 down payment (with $3500 loan).

Q: Which should I buy first, car or house?
A: HOUSE FIRST! You cannot sleep without a house, which means:
   • You can't restore energy (death inevitable)
   • You can't progress in the game
   • House is mandatory to survive

Q: Can I buy both at once?
A: Depends on your money. Typically, buy house first (day 2-4), then car later 
   (day 10-15) once you've earned more income.

Q: How do I buy an asset?
A: From Main Menu → Asset Operations → Choose to Buy Car or House → Confirm 
   purchase (if you have funds).

Q: What are the purchase conditions?
A:
   a) Simcoin ≥ Full Price → Buy immediately (no loan needed)
   b) Down Payment ≤ Simcoin AND Remaining Loan ≤ $5000 → Buy with loan
   c) Otherwise → Cannot buy (insufficient funds)

Q: Can I sell my house or car?
A: Yes. From Main Menu → Asset Operations → Sell Car/House → Receive 
   depreciated value.

Q: How much do I get when I sell?
A: Depreciated value:
   • Car: 60% of purchase price ($2000 car sells for $1200)
   • House: 90% of purchase price ($5000 house sells for $4500)

Q: Do I need to pay off the loan before selling?
A: YES! You must clear any associated loan first. Loan deducted from sale 
   proceeds automatically.

Q: What if I can't afford the full down payment?
A: You cannot buy without the down payment. Save money first, or approach a 
   career that pays better.

Q: Can I own multiple cars or houses?
A: The current system appears to support one of each. Owning a second property 
   would require different mechanics not yet implemented.

Q: What's depreciation?
A: It's the loss of value over time. Assets you purchase are worth less if sold:
   • Car loses 40% value
   • House loses 10% value
   • This represents wear and tear

F. LOCATIONS & TRAVEL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What locations can I visit?
A:
   1. Street - Starting location (limited actions)
   2. Park - Recreation and play
   3. Bank - Financial transactions
   4. Restaurant - Food and work
   5. Hospital - Health and work
   6. Home - Sleep, shower, rest (must own)

Q: Where do I start the game?
A: On a random Street on Day 1. From there, you travel to other locations.

Q: How do I travel to a location?
A: From Main Menu → "Travel to Location" → Select your destination → Confirm. 
   Takes 1 hour and affects your Sim.

Q: What's the difference between walking and driving?
A:
   WALKING:
   • Energy cost: -15 per trip
   • Hunger cost: +10 per hunger
   • Speed: 1 hour
   
   DRIVING (requires car):
   • Energy cost: -5 per trip
   • Hunger cost: +0
   • Speed: 1 hour
   • Uses car (doesn't deplete it)

Q: Can I fast travel?
A: No, all travel takes 1 hour regardless of distance or method. Plan accordingly!

Q: What can I do at each location?

STREET (Starting location):
   • Limited actions available
   • Low income opportunities
   • Free to exist here

PARK:
   • Play (improve mood)
   • Socialize
   • Recreation activities

BANK:
   • Deposit money
   • Withdraw money
   • Apply for loans
   • Repay loans

RESTAURANT:
   • Eat (reduce hunger)
   • Work as Chef (if employed)
   • Different meal options

HOSPITAL:
   • Get checkup (medical)
   • Work as Doctor (if employed)
   • Emergency services

HOME (once purchased):
   • Sleep (restore energy, advance day)
   • Shower (improve hygiene)
   • Use toilet (reduce bladder)
   • Rest (general wellness)
   • Relax

Q: Can I visit the same location twice in one day?
A: Yes, you can visit any location as many times as you want, but each visit 
   takes 1 hour.

Q: What happens if I run out of time to travel?
A: If it's late in the day, you might not have time to travel and return home 
   before 22:00 (forced sleep time). Plan your schedule carefully!

Q: Do I need a house to live in the game?
A: Yes! Without a house, you cannot sleep, which means you cannot restore Energy 
   and will eventually die. Housing is mandatory.

Q: What if I sell my house?
A: You'll have the money but nowhere to sleep. This is a bad idea! Always have 
   housing before selling your house.

Q: Can I own a house and a car at the same time?
A: Absolutely! In fact, it's optimal to own both. The car helps you manage 
   energy costs, and the house is non-negotiable.

Q: Do locations have limited hours?
A: No, all locations are available all day. However, you might be forced to 
   sleep at 22:00 regardless of location.

G. SAVING & LOADING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: How do I save my game?
A: The game saves automatically when you quit via the Quit Game option in any 
   menu. Look for "Save in progress..." messages.

Q: Can I save mid-game without quitting?
A: No, automatic saves only happen on quit. However, you can return to the Start 
   Menu and it preserves your game state.

Q: How do I load a saved game?
A: From the Start Menu, select "Continue Game" to load your last saved position.

Q: Can I have multiple save files?
A: The current system uses one save slot. If you want multiple games, you must 
   choose "New Game" (which overwrites the previous save).

Q: Where is the save file located?
A: The save file is likely stored in the project directory as "savegame.txt" 
   or similar.

Q: What happens if I quit without saving?
A: Using "Quit Game" from any menu saves automatically. If you force-quit the 
   application, you may lose progress.

Q: Can I export my save file?
A: The game supports textual save files. You can backup "savegame.txt" from 
   the project directory.

Q: What does "Reset all on New Game" mean?
A: Choosing "New Game" completely resets your progress - all Sims, money, 
   assets, and game state are deleted. You start fresh.

Q: Is there a way to undo actions?
A: No, there's no undo feature. All actions are permanent, so think carefully 
   before making major decisions!

H. TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: My Sim died! How do I continue?
A: When a Sim dies, they're removed from the game. You can:
   1. Continue with other Sims (if you have multiple)
   2. Create new Sims from Sims Management Menu
   3. Start a "New Game" if all Sims are dead

Q: Why can't I buy a house?
A: Possible reasons:
   • Insufficient funds (need $5000 or $1500 + loan availability)
   • Loan limit reached (max $5000 total)
   • Already own a house (system might limit to one)

Q: Why can't I get a job?
A: Possible reasons:
   • Already employed in another job
   • That job unavailable for your Sim type (age)
   • Location-specific jobs require being at that location

Q: My money disappeared!
A: You likely:
   • Spent it on purchases (house, car, meals)
   • Paid loans or down payments
   • Money deducted for travel/activities
   • Review your status history to track spending

Q: Why does my Sim keep dying?
A: Most likely causes:
   1. Needs reaching zero (especially hunger/energy)
   2. Forgetting to sleep (forced at 22:00, but energy depletes fast)
   3. Loan default (after day 80 if debt exceeds assets)

   Solution: Check needs constantly, sleep daily, manage loans carefully.

Q: Can't find an expected location?
A: Some locations might be:
   • Unlocked after certain conditions
   • Available only after purchasing assets
   • Not yet implemented in the build
   
   Refer to the in-game menus for available locations.

Q: Why does the menu look different?
A: The game might display:
   • Different options based on your assets (owns car/house?)
   • Different actions available per location
   • Context-specific menus based on your Sim's state

Q: Is there a walkthrough?
A: Refer to TUTORIAL.txt for detailed step-by-step guidance!

Q: I'm stuck, what do I do?
A: 
   1. Check your current needs (especially red ones)
   2. Head to nearest location that addresses critical needs
   3. If Sim is jobless, get a job ASAP
   4. If homeless, make house your priority
   5. Read TUTORIAL.txt for detailed guidance

I. ADVANCED MECHANICS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What is the in-game calendar?
A: The game tracks:
   • Days (Days 1, 2, 3, etc.)
   • Hours (08:00 to 22:00, 24-hour format)
   • Days reset at midnight (22:00 → next day 08:00)
   • All Sims advance together

Q: What happens on special days?
A: The game doesn't currently feature special days or holidays. Every day 
   follows the same schedule.

Q: Can I speed up time?
A: No, each action advances time by 1 hour naturally. You can't skip ahead or 
   pause time.

Q: How does skill progression work?
A: 
   • Each job starts at skill level 1
   • Repeated work sessions increase skill slowly
   • Levels go from 1-10
   • Higher levels unlock higher salaries
   • Skill progression is gradual (weeks of work to advance)

Q: Are there hidden stat systems?
A: Yes, there's a complex system being tracked including:
   • Individual need tracking
   • Job experience/skill levels
   • Asset ownership data
   • Loan/financial data
   • Location history
   • Age-based modifiers

Q: What age types exist?
A: Three Sim types:
   • Child Sim - Different income/needs scaling
   • Adult Sim - Standard gameplay
   • Elder Sim - May have different mechanics/wisdom bonuses

Q: Do different Sims age?
A: The current system classifies Sims by type but aging mechanics may be limited.

Q: Can Sims interact with each other?
A: Sims can share resources (money, house, car) but direct interactions appear 
   limited in the console version.

Q: Is there a relationship system?
A: Not currently visible. Sims can coexist in the same household but 
   relationships aren't tracked in detail.

Q: What's the maximum game length?
A: No maximum. You can play indefinitely across multiple in-game years!

Q: Is there a time limit to objectives?
A: No hard time limits, but:
   • Loans have day 60 (repossession) and day 80 (death) limits
   • Otherwise, play at your own pace

Q: Can I play forever?
A: Theoretically yes, but game-overs occur from:
   • Sim death (needs zero or loan default)
   • Running out of Sims (all dead)
   
   Manage carefully to sustain length!

J. STRATEGY TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEGINNER STRATEGY (Days 1-10):
   1. Create a Sim, immediately get a job
   2. Work repeatedly to build $1500 for house
   3. Buy house ASAP (essential for sleep)
   4. Work more for additional income
   5. Establish daily routine: Work → Eat → Rest → Sleep

INTERMEDIATE STRATEGY (Days 10-30):
   1. You have a house, stable income
   2. Start saving for car ($400 down, $1600 loan)
   3. Once car acquired, travel costs drop significantly
   4. Build skill levels (higher pay)
   5. Accumulate bank savings ($5000+)

ADVANCED STRATEGY (Day 30+):
   1. Multiple job options if desired (switch for better pay)
   2. Explore higher-paying careers as skills max out
   3. Create additional Sims (share house/car)
   4. Coordinate household operation
   5. Build wealth ($20,000+ in assets + savings)

WEALTH BUILDING:
   • Monthly income: $1000-$2000+ per Sim per in-game month
   • Minimize unnecessary spending
   • Use car to reduce travel energy costs
   • Bank extra funds for security
   • Plan major purchases

DAILY OPTIMIZATION:
   • Minimize travel (plan location visits)
   • Stack activities (eat at work location if possible)
   • Sleep early to avoid forced sleep at 22:00
   • Schedule jobs during optimal hours
   • Keep needs in yellow to minimize actions

AVOIDING DEATH:
   ✓ Monitor needs daily (check status often)
   ✓ Maintain green status on critical needs
   ✓ Sleep every night before 22:00
   ✓ Eat regularly (hunger kills fast)
   ✓ Own a house (no house = no sleep = death)
   ✓ Manage loans (avoid day 80 default)
   ✓ Build emergency fund ($2000+ in bank)

JOB SELECTION:
   • Chef: Steady income, work-life balance
   • Doctor: Higher pay, important looking career
   • Engineer: Strong income potential
   • Influencer: Unique career path
   • Jobless: Maximum free time (no income though)

ASSET PRIORITY:
   1. House (mandatory)
   2. Car (reduces travel costs)
   3. Nest egg savings (emergency fund)

COMMON MISTAKES:
   ✗ Ignoring needs until critical
   ✗ Not sleeping daily (you WILL be forced)
   ✗ Overspending on non-essentials
   ✗ Taking loans without repayment plan
   ✗ Selling house without replacement
   ✗ Not saving income
   ✗ Forgetting to eat

SUCCESS INDICATORS:
   ✓ Stable daily income (job acquired)
   ✓ House acquired (sleeping regularly)
   ✓ Needs maintained in yellow/green
   ✓ Growing savings account
   ✓ Skill levels increasing
   ✓ Car acquired (travel efficiency)
