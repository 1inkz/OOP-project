## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Got questions? Good. Your Sim probably does too.

This FAQ is here for quick answers about gameplay, money, assets, needs, travel, and survival.

---

## A. General Questions

**Q: What kind of game is this?**
A: It’s a text-based life simulation game where you manage Sims, their needs, jobs, money, assets, and daily decisions.

**Q: Is there a win condition?**
A: Not exactly. The game is open-ended. You can set your own goals, like getting rich, maxing a career, buying a house, or keeping multiple Sims alive.

**Q: How do I lose?**
A: A Sim dies if any need reaches 0. If all Sims die, the game ends.

**Q: Can I play with more than one Sim?**
A: Yes. You can create multiple Sims, but only one is controlled at a time.

**Q: How do I switch Sims?**
A: Go back to the Sims Management Menu and select another existing Sim.

---

## B. Needs and Survival

**Q: What needs do I have to manage?**
A: Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, and Mood.

**Q: What do the colors mean?**
A:

* Green = good
* Yellow = warning
* Red = critical

**Q: What happens if a need becomes 0?**
A: Your Sim dies instantly.

**Q: How do I restore needs?**
A: Use location-based actions:

* Hunger → eat at Restaurant
* Energy → sleep at Home
* Hygiene → shower at Home
* Bladder → use toilet at Home
* Mood → play or relax at Park

**Q: Do needs change automatically?**
A: Needs change as time passes. Since actions take time, your needs will keep shifting during gameplay.

**Q: Can I ignore sleep?**
A: No. At night the game warns you, and at 22:00 your Sim will faint and move to the next day.

---

## C. Time and Daily Flow

**Q: What time does the day start and end?**
A: The day starts at 08:00 and ends at 22:00.

**Q: How much time does one action use?**
A: Every action uses 1 in-game hour.

**Q: Does traveling also take time?**
A: Yes. Traveling also costs 1 hour.

**Q: What happens when I sleep?**
A: Your Sim moves to the next day.

**Q: Do all Sims move to the next day together?**
A: Yes. The world stays synchronized.

---

## D. Jobs and Money

**Q: How do I get a job?**
A: From the Main Menu, choose **Find Job / Change Job**.

**Q: What jobs are available?**
A: Chef, Doctor, Engineer, Influencer, and Jobless.

**Q: How do I earn money?**
A: The main way is by getting a job and working.

**Q: Can I have more than one job?**
A: No. One Sim can only hold one job at a time.

**Q: How do I improve my pay?**
A: Work more often to increase your skill level. Higher skill usually means better earnings.

**Q: What does Jobless do?**
A: No income, but no job responsibilities either.

---

## E. Bank, Simcoin, and Loans

**Q: What’s the difference between Simcoin and bank money?**
A: Simcoin is money on hand. Bank money is stored in your account.

**Q: How do I deposit or withdraw money?**
A: Go to the Bank and choose Deposit or Withdraw.

**Q: What is the maximum loan?**
A: $5000.

**Q: Where does loan money go?**
A: Directly into your Simcoin.

**Q: How do I repay a loan?**
A: Go to the Bank and choose Repay Loan. Repayment is deducted from Simcoin.

**Q: What happens if I ignore my loan for too long?**
A: You risk repossession after day 60, and death after day 80 if your funds are still too low.

---

## F. House and Car

**Q: What assets can I buy?**
A: A car and a house.

**Q: How much does a car cost?**
A: $2000 full price, or $400 down payment with loan support.

**Q: How much does a house cost?**
A: $5000 full price, or $1500 down payment with loan support.

**Q: Which should I buy first?**
A: House first. It is much more important for survival.

**Q: Why is the house so important?**
A: Because Home gives you access to key actions like sleeping, showering, and toilet use.

**Q: What does the car do?**
A: It makes travel less costly on your needs.

**Q: Can I sell assets?**
A: Yes, but they sell at reduced value.

**Q: What are the resale values?**
A:

* Car = 60% of purchase value
* House = 90% of purchase value

**Q: Can I sell if I still have a loan?**
A: You must clear the loan first.

---

## G. Travel and Locations

**Q: Where do Sims start?**
A: On a random street.

**Q: What locations can I visit?**
A: Street, Park, Bank, Restaurant, Hospital, and Home.

**Q: What’s the difference between walking and driving?**
A: Walking costs more Energy and Hunger. Driving is more efficient, but you need a car.

**Q: Does driving save time?**
A: No. Both walking and driving still take 1 hour.

**Q: Can I travel as many times as I want in one day?**
A: Yes, as long as you still have enough time and your needs can handle it.

**Q: What happens if I’m far from home at night?**
A: You still risk reaching the forced sleep time at 22:00, so plan carefully.

---

## H. Saving and Loading

**Q: How do I save the game?**
A: The game saves when you choose **Quit Game**.

**Q: How do I load the game again?**
A: Use **Continue Game** from the Start Menu.

**Q: Does New Game keep my old progress?**
A: No. New Game resets everything.

**Q: Can I have multiple save files?**
A: Not in the current system. It uses one save state.

## I. Common Problems

**Q: Why can't I run my code?
A: Run these commands 
* "cd ../bin"
* "java simscli.Main"

B: For visual studio code error:
Ctrl Shift P and Run "Java: Clean Java Language Server Workspace" then Restart VS code

**Q: Why can’t I buy a house or car?**
A: Usually because:

* you don’t have enough Simcoin
* you don’t have enough for the down payment
* your loan limit is already too high

**Q: Why does my Sim keep dying?**
A: Most likely because a need reached 0, or you failed to manage sleep, hunger, or debt.

**Q: Why can’t I sleep?**
A: You need access to Home.

**Q: Why can’t I work?**
A: Make sure your Sim has a job and is at the correct location for that job.

**Q: Why did my asset disappear?**
A: It may have been repossessed due to unpaid loan conditions.

## J. Quick Tips

**Q: What should I do first in a new game?**
A: Get a job, earn money, and work toward buying a house.

**Q: Should I get a car early?**
A: Only after your house situation is secure.

**Q: What is the safest strategy?**
A: Keep needs out of red, avoid bad loans, and always plan your time before night.

**Q: What should I check often?**
A: Your status screen. It helps prevent stupid deaths.
