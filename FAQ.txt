# ❓ FAQ – QUICK HELP

## 🧠 GENERAL

**Q: What is this game?**
A: A terminal-based life simulation where you manage Sims, their needs, money, assets, and time.

**Q: Is there a fixed win condition?**
A: Nope! 🎯 Success = survival + stability + growth.

**Q: How do I lose?**
A: If Hunger reaches 0 → 💀 instant death
Other needs can trigger serious penalties (hospital, burnout, accidents).

---

## ❤️ NEEDS

**Q: What needs exist?**
A:
- Hunger 🍔
- Energy ⚡
- Hygiene 🚿
- Social 💬
- Fun 🎮
- Bladder 🚽

**Q: Why am I struggling even if I’m not dead?**
A: Critical needs trigger punishment events like:
- Forced sleep 😴
- Accidents 💥
- Hospital time 🏥

---

## ⏰ TIME

**Q: Why does the game feel fast?**
A: Time moves constantly — actions + travel consume time while needs keep decreasing.

**Q: What happens at night?**
A: 🌙 Energy drains faster, and you’ll get a midnight warning.

---

## 💼 JOBS

**Q: How do I earn money reliably?**
A: Get a job and work at the correct locations.

**Q: What jobs are available?**
A:
- 👨‍🍳 Chef
- 🩺 Doctor
- 🏦 Bank Teller
- 📱 Influencer
- 🚫 Jobless

---

## 💰 MONEY & LOANS

**Q: Should I take loans early?**
A: Only if you have a plan 💡 — loans help, but they can trap you.

**Q: What happens if I ignore loans?**
A: Penalties escalate:
- Interest increases 📈
- Assets may be taken 🏚
- Situation becomes harder to recover

---

## 🏡 ASSETS

**Q: What should I buy first?**
A: 🏠 A house — it unlocks essential survival actions.

**Q: Why buy a car?**
A: 🚗 Faster, less tiring travel (but requires maintenance).

---

## 🎰 CASINO

**Q: Can every Sim enter the casino?**
A: ❌ No — Child Sims are not allowed.

---

**Q: What can I do there?**
A:
- 🎰 Slot Machine
- 🃏 Blackjack (21)
- 🚽 Use Toilet
- 🍿 Eat Snack

---

**Q: Slot machine payouts?**
A:
- 🎰 777 → 25x
- 🎰 Other triple → 10x
- 🎰 Any pair → 2x
- ❌ No match → lose

---

**Q: Blackjack payout rules?**
A:
- Normal win → 2x
- Double-down win → 4x
- Natural blackjack → 3x

---

**Q: What is Double Down?**
A: You double your bet, get **1 extra card**, then auto-stand.

---

**Q: What is Five-card Charlie?**
A: If you have 5 cards and total ≤ 21 → instant win 🎉

---
