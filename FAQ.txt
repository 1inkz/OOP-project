❓ FAQ – QUICK HELP

---

🧠 GENERAL

Q: What is this game?
A: A life simulation where you try not to die while managing everything.

Q: Is there a win?
A: No fixed win. You survive and improve.

Q: How do I lose?
A: Any need hits 0 → 💀

---

🍔 NEEDS

Q: What needs do I have?
A:
- Hunger
- Energy
- Hygiene
- Social
- Fun
- Bladder

Q: Why do I keep dying?
A:
- ignored needs
- ignored sleep
- bad time management

---

⏰ TIME

Q: Why is time so hard?
A: Because EVERYTHING costs time.

Q: What happens at night?
A:
20:00 → reminder  
21:00 → warning  
22:00 → forced sleep  

---

💼 JOBS

Q: How do I earn money?
A: Get a job and work.

Q: Jobs available?
A:
- Chef
- Doctor
- Bank Teller
- Influencer

---

💳 MONEY & LOANS

Q: Should I take loans?
A: Only if you know what you’re doing.

Q: What happens if I ignore loans?
A:

- worse consequences later

---

🏠 ASSETS

Q: Best first asset?
A: 🏠 House

Q: Why?
A: It unlocks survival actions.

---

🌍 LOCATIONS

Q: Why can’t I go Home?
A: You don’t own a house yet.

Q: Why do locations matter?
A: Different actions only exist in certain places.

🐶 PETS
