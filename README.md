# 🎮 THE SIMS - TERMINAL CONSOLE EDITION

A Java-based life simulation game you play directly in the terminal.  
Manage your Sims, survive daily life, earn money, and build your dream lifestyle!

---

## 🧠 Overview

Create and manage multiple Sims, but control one at a time.

### 🎯 Your Goal
- ❤️ Stay alive
- 💰 Stay financially stable
- 🏡 Build your lifestyle (house, car, hotel, pets)

---

## ⚙️ Core Systems

### 👤 Sims
Each Sim has:
- Needs (must stay above 0!)
- Job and career progression
- 💵 Cash, 🏦 bank deposits, and 💳 loans
- 🚗 Assets (car, house, hotel)
- 🐶 Pets
- 📍 Current location

---

## 🧭: UNDERSTAND THE MAIN MENU

Key options:
- 👀 View Status
- 🌍 Travel to Location
- ⚡ View Current Location Actions
- 💼 Job Management
- 🏡 Asset Operations
- ⏰ Pass Time (1 hour)

💡 Tip:
**View Status = your survival dashboard. Check it often.**

---

### ❤️ Needs System

Track and manage:
- Hunger 🍔
- Energy ⚡
- Hygiene 🚿
- Social 💬
- Fun 🎮
- Bladder 🚽

#### 📊 Need Levels
- 🟢 60+ → Good
- 🟡 21–59 → Warning
- 🔴 0–20 → Critical

⚠️ Important:
- Hunger / Energy at 0 = 💀 death
- Other needs may trigger penalties (hospital, burnout, accidents)

---

### ⏰ Time System
- Most actions take **1 in-game hour**
- Travel also consumes time and affects needs
- 🌙 Late nights drain energy faster (midnight warning!)

---

Available jobs:
- 👨‍🍳 Chef

💡 Work to earn money and level up
⚠️ Overworking can damage your needs

- 🎰 Any pair → **2x**
- ❌ No match → lose bet

---

### 🃏 Blackjack (21)

- Start with 2 cards
- Options: Hit / Stand / Double
- Double = 1 extra card + auto stand

#### 💰 Payouts
- Natural blackjack → **3x**
- Normal win → **2x**
- Double-down win → **4x**
- Five-card Charlie (≤21) → instant win 🎉

## 🧠 GENERAL

**Q: What is this game?**
A: A terminal-based life simulation where you manage Sims, their needs, money, assets, and time.

**Q: Is there a fixed win condition?**
A: Nope! 🎯 Success = survival + stability + growth.

**Q: How do I lose?**
A: If Hunger reaches 0 → 💀 instant death
Other needs can trigger serious penalties (hospital, burnout, accidents).

---

## ❤️ NEEDS

**Q: What needs exist?**
A:
- Hunger 🍔
- Energy ⚡
- Hygiene 🚿
- Social 💬
- Fun 🎮
- Bladder 🚽

**Q: Why am I struggling even if I’m not dead?**
A: Critical needs trigger punishment events like:
- Forced sleep 😴
- Accidents 💥
- Hospital time 🏥

---

## ⏰ TIME

**Q: Why does the game feel fast?**
A: Time moves constantly — actions + travel consume time while needs keep decreasing.

**Q: What happens at night?**
A: 🌙 Energy drains faster, and you’ll get a midnight warning.

---

## 💼 JOBS

**Q: How do I earn money reliably?**
A: Get a job and work at the correct locations.

**Q: What jobs are available?**
A:
- 👨‍🍳 Chef
- 🩺 Doctor
- 🏦 Bank Teller
- 📱 Influencer
- 🚫 Jobless

---

## 💰 MONEY & LOANS

**Q: Should I take loans early?**

**Q: What happens if I ignore loans?**
A: Penalties escalate:

- Situation becomes harder to recover
