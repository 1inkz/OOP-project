1. GETTING STARTED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When you start the game, you'll see the Start Menu with three options:
  • New Game - Start a fresh game with new Sims
  • Continue Game - Load your previously saved game
  • Quit Game - Exit the application

Your first time playing? Choose "New Game"!


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. CREATING YOUR FIRST SIM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After selecting New Game, you'll enter the Sims Management Menu:
  1. Select "Create New Sims"
  2. You can create Sims of different ages:
     • Child Sim - Lower income from jobs, different skill development
     • Adult Sim - Standard gameplay with full job options
     • Elder Sim - Can have wisdom bonuses, different needs

  3. Enter your Sim's name
  4. Select the Sim you just created to enter the Main Menu

TIP: Creating multiple Sims allows complex household interactions and 
     shared resources. You can manage them one at a time!

3. UNDERSTANDING THE MAIN MENU
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Once inside your Sim's menu, you'll see options like:

  1) View [Name] status
     └─ Shows your Sim's current status:
        • Current time (Day and Hour)
        • All needs levels (represented by status bars)
        • Current job and level
        • Assets owned (car/house)
        • Money on hand (Simcoin)
        • Loan balance
        
  2) Travel to Location
     └─ Choose where your Sim will go (see Section 7)
     
  3) View [Location] Action Menu
     └─ Perform location-specific actions
     
  4) Find Job / Change Job
     └─ Get employment or switch jobs (see Section 5)
     
  5) Asset Operations [Buy/Sell Car/House]
     └─ Purchase or sell vehicles and properties (see Section 6)
     
  6) Pass Time (1 Hour)
     └─ Advance time by 1 hour. ALL ACTIONS cost 1 hour in this game!
     
  7) View FAQ
     └─ Quick reference guide for gameplay questions
     
  8) Back [To Sims Management Menu]
  9) Quit Game

4. MANAGING YOUR SIM'S NEEDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Your Sim has several needs that must be maintained:
  • Hunger - Increases when not eating
  • Energy - Decreases from physical activities
  • Hygiene - Decreases from daily activities
  • Bladder - Increases over time
  • Mood - Affected by needs and activities

NEED STATUS COLORS:
  • Green (≥60) - Excellent, no action needed
  • Yellow (21-59) - Warning, address soon
  • Red (≤20) - Critical, needs immediate attention!

WHAT HAPPENS IF A NEED REACHES ZERO:
  Your Sim will DIE instantly! Game Over!

BASIC NEED MANAGEMENT:
  1. Check your status regularly (View [Name] status)
  2. Visit locations to fulfill needs:
     • Restaurant → Eat (reduces Hunger)
     • Home → Sleep (restores Energy)
     • Home → Shower (improves Hygiene)
     • Home → Toilet (reduces Bladder)
     • Park → Play (improves Mood)

SLEEP SCHEDULE:
  • 20:00 (8 PM) - Sleep reminder message
  • 21:00 (9 PM) - Sleep warning message
  • 22:00 (10 PM) - Forced faint (your Sim collapses to sleep)
  
  NOTE: After sleeping, your Sim advances to the next day!

DAILY SCHEDULE REMINDER:
  • Day starts at 08:00 (8 AM)
  • Day ends at 22:00 (10 PM)
  • All Sims advance to the next day together

5. MAKING MONEY & GETTING A JOB
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You start with a small amount of Simcoin. To earn more money, you need a job!

AVAILABLE JOBS:
  1) Chef - Earn Simcoin through cooking
  2) Doctor - Earn Simcoin through healthcare
  3) Engineer - Earn Simcoin through technical work
  4) Influencer - Earn Simcoin through social media
  5) Jobless - No income, but no job stress

HOW TO GET A JOB:
  1. From Main Menu, select "Find Job / Change Job"
  2. Choose your desired job from the list
  3. Once hired, you earn money each time you work
  4. Jobs are location-based (find them at appropriate locations)

JOB LEVELS:
  • Each job has a skill level (1-10)
  • Higher skill = higher pay per work session
  • Gain skill by repeatedly performing job actions
  • Progress visible in your status menu

INCOME TIPS:
  • Different jobs offer different salary rates
  • You can change jobs anytime (no penalty)
  • Jobless gives no income but gives more free time
  • Career progression through skill levels increases earnings

6. MANAGING ASSETS (CARS & HOUSES)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Assets are major purchases that provide convenience and can increase in value.

BASIC CAR:
  • Cost: $2000 (full purchase) or $400 (down payment with loan)
  • Benefit: Faster travel between locations
  • Travel cost: Consume time but use less energy
  • Depreciation: Sells for 60% of purchase value

BASIC HOUSE:
  • Cost: $5000 (full purchase) or $1500 (down payment with loan)
  • Benefit: Place to sleep, shower, use toilet, rest
  • Depreciation: Sells for 90% of purchase value

HOW TO BUY AN ASSET:
  From Main Menu → Asset Operations → Choose Buy Car or Buy House
  
  Purchase Rules (in order of priority):
  a) If you have enough Simcoin for full price → Can buy immediately
  b) If you have down payment AND loan available → Can buy with loan
  c) Otherwise → Cannot buy (insufficient funds)
  
  LOAN LIMITS:
  • Maximum total loan: $5000
  • Down payment required if using loan

HOW TO SELL AN ASSET:
  From Main Menu → Asset Operations → Choose Sell Car or Sell House
  
  Before selling:
  1. Must pay off any associated loan first
  2. You'll receive: Purchase Value × Depreciation Rate
  3. Proceeds go to your Simcoin account

DEPRECIATION:
  • Car: 60% value (sell for $1200 if bought at $2000)
  • House: 90% value (sell for $4500 if bought at $5000)

ASSET TIPS:
  • House is essential for sleep (can't sleep on street!)
  • Car reduces travel energy costs
  • Start with house if choosing between the two
  • Selling assets with loans: clear debt before selling

7. BANKING & LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Banking System helps you manage money and debts.

ACCOUNT TYPES:
  • Simcoin (on-hand cash) - Easy to spend, visible balance
  • Bank Account - Secure savings

BANKING ACTIONS:
  1. Deposit - Move money from hand to bank (earn interest)
  2. Withdraw - Move money from bank to hand (spend freely)
  3. Apply Loan - Borrow up to $5000 for purchases
  4. Repay Loan - Pay back borrowed money (from Simcoin)

LOAN MECHANICS:
  • Max Loan: $5000 total
  • Usage: Typically for down payments on cars/houses
  • Interest: Accumulates over time (check daily!)
  • Repayment: Deducted from Simcoin only

LOAN DEFAULT CONSEQUENCES:
  After day 80:
  • If (Bank Deposit + Simcoin) < Loan Amount → Your Sim DIES!
  
  This is a fail condition, so manage loans carefully!

REPOSSESSION DETAILS:
  After day 60:
  • If Loan > $2000:
    - With house AND car → Lose house, loan cleared
    - Without house → Lose car, loan cleared
  • If Loan < $2000:
    - Loss priority: Car first, then house
    - Loan is cleared upon repossession

BANKING TIPS:
  • Keep an eye on your loan balance
  • Deposit extra money to be safe
  • Early repayment is always smart
  • Plan asset purchases to minimize long-term debt

8. TRAVELING TO LOCATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Your Sim starts on a random street on Day 1. To accomplish things, you must 
travel to different locations.

AVAILABLE LOCATIONS:
  1. Street - Starting location, limited actions
  2. Park - Recreation and mood improvement
  3. Bank - Money and loan operations
  4. Restaurant - Eating to reduce hunger
  5. Hospital - Medical checkups and health services
  6. Home - Sleep, shower, personal hygiene, rest

TRAVEL METHODS:
  
  WITHOUT A CAR (Walking):
    • Energy cost: -15 per travel
    • Hunger increase: +10 per travel
    • Takes same time as driving (1 hour)
  
  WITH A CAR (Driving):
    • Energy cost: -5 per travel
    • Hunger increase: +0 per travel
    • Takes same time as walking (1 hour)
  
  TRAVEL TIPS:
    • Car makes frequent travel much more feasible
    • Always account for energy/hunger when planning trips
    • Multiple locations = multiple travels = multiple energy costs

LOCATION SELECTION MENU:
  When traveling, you'll see current status:
  • Current Location: Where you are now
  • Own Car: Yes/No (affects travel availability)
  • Own House: Yes/No (affects available actions)
  • Time remaining in day
  
  Choose your destination and press Enter!

9. WORKING WITH LOCATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Each location offers unique actions. Here's what you can do at each one:

STREET:
  • Limited actions available
  • Good starting point
  • Walk to other locations

PARK:
  • Play - Improves mood and recreation
  • Walk dogs/pets (if owned)
  • Relax outdoors

BANK:
  • Deposit money to bank account
  • Withdraw money for spending
  • Apply for loans
  • Repay existing loans

RESTAURANT:
  • Eat - Reduces hunger
  • Work as Chef (if employed)
  • Different meal options available

HOSPITAL:
  • Get checkup - Maintains health
  • Work as Doctor (if employed)
  • Emergency medical services

HOME (Once purchased):
  • Sleep - Restores energy (moves to next day!)
  • Shower - Improves hygiene
  • Use Toilet - Reduces bladder need
  • Rest - General wellness
  • Work on career goals
  
  NOTE: Homeless Sims cannot sleep properly!

PET STORE:
  • Buy pets (cats, dogs, bunnies)
  • Pet supplies and care

LOCATION ACTION TIPS:
  • Each action takes 1 hour
  • Plan your daily schedule carefully
  • Some actions require specific items/conditions
  • Job-related actions vary by job type

10. ADVANCED TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OPTIMAL DAILY SCHEDULE EXAMPLE (Adult Sim):
  08:00 - Wake up, check status
  09:00 - Work at job (1 hour)
  10:00 - Work at job (1 hour)
  11:00 - Eat at restaurant
  12:00 - Work at job (1 hour)
  13:00 - Travel to park
  14:00 - Relax at park (mood boost)
  15:00 - Travel home
  16:00 - Rest at home
  17:00 - Work at job (1 hour)
  18:00 - Eat at restaurant
  19:00 - Travel home
  20:00 - (Sleep warning)
  21:00 - Shower at home
  22:00 - Sleep at home (advances to next day)

SURVIVAL STRATEGIES:
  1. Prioritize house purchase (you MUST sleep)
  2. Get a job early (income is essential)
  3. Monitor all needs - red needs are deadly!
  4. Reserve evening hours for sleep
  5. Keep loan balance low
  6. Save regularly (Quit Game saves automatically)

SKILL PROGRESSION:
  • Repeated job actions increase skill level
  • Higher skills = higher pay
  • Takes multiple days/weeks of work to advance
  • Can't skip the grind - career progression is gradual

MONEY MANAGEMENT:
  • Start small - basic needs first
  • Save for house down payment ($1500)
  • Car is secondary to house
  • Banking offers safety for excess funds
  • Plan major purchases weeks in advance

MULTI-SIM HOUSEHOLDS:
  • Create multiple Sims to share resources
  • Only one Sim is active at a time
  • Shared assets (house, car, money in storage)
  • Switch between Sims from management menu
  • Coordinate schedules and responsibilities

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
  ✗ Ignoring red-level needs (instant death!)
  ✗ Forgetting to sleep (forced at 22:00)
  ✗ Overspending on non-essentials
  ✗ Taking huge loans (repossession risk)
  ✗ Not visiting locations with your Sim
  ✗ Forgetting to save progress regularly
