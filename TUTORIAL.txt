🎮 TUTORIAL – START HERE

Welcome to The Sims (Terminal Edition)!

This guide helps you survive your FIRST few days without dying instantly 💀

---

🧍 STEP 1: CREATE YOUR SIM

Go to:
Sims Menu → Create New Sim

- pick a type
- enter a name
- select your Sim

---

📋 STEP 2: UNDERSTAND YOUR MENU

Main menu controls EVERYTHING:

👀 View Status → CHECK THIS OFTEN  
✈️ Travel → move around  
📍 Location Actions → do stuff  
💼 Job → earn money  
🏠 Assets → buy things  
⏰ Pass Time → advance game  

---

🍔 STEP 3: DO NOT DIE (NEEDS)

Your needs:
- Hunger 🍔
- Energy 😴
- Hygiene 🚿
- Social 🗣
- Fun 🎮
- Bladder 🚽

💀 ANY = 0 → death

---

⏰ STEP 4: TIME IS YOUR ENEMY

- actions cost time
- travel costs time
- needs decay over time

🌙 Night:
20:00 → reminder  
21:00 → warning  
22:00 → forced sleep  

---

💼 STEP 5: GET A JOB

No job = no money = you suffer

Pick:
- Chef
- Doctor
- Bank Teller
- Influencer

👉 Work → earn → survive

---

🏠 STEP 6: GET A HOUSE

This is HUGE:

Without house ❌  
- no proper sleep  
- no shower  
- no toilet  

👉 House = survival unlocked

---

💳 STEP 7: LOANS (BE CAREFUL)

Loans help you early…

BUT:
👉 ignore them → bad things happen

---

🐶 STEP 8: PETS (ADVANCED MODE)

Pets are:
- fun 🎾
- extra responsibility 😅

👉 Don’t get pets too early

---

🧠 SAFE START STRATEGY

1. Get job 💼  
2. Monitor needs 👀  
3. Don’t waste time ⏰  
4. Save money 💰  
5. Buy house 🏠  
6. THEN expand  

---

❌ COMMON MISTAKES

- ignoring needs  
- working too much  
- taking too many loans  
- forgetting time  
- buying assets too early  

---

🏁 FINAL TIP

Think before every action.

This game rewards:
👉 planning > rushing
