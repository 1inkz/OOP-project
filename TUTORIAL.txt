# 📘 TUTORIAL – START HERE

Welcome to **The Sims (Terminal Edition)** 🎮

This guide will help you survive your first few days and avoid costly mistakes.

---

## 🧱 STEP 1: CREATE & SELECT A SIM

Go to:
- **Sims Menu → Create New Sim**

Choose:
- Sim type (Child / Adult / Elder)
- Name

Then select the Sim you want to control.

---

## 🧭 STEP 2: UNDERSTAND THE MAIN MENU

Key options:
- 👀 View Status
- 🌍 Travel to Location
- ⚡ View Current Location Actions
- 💼 Job Management
- 🏡 Asset Operations
- ⏰ Pass Time (1 hour)

💡 Tip:
**View Status = your survival dashboard. Check it often.**

---

## ❤️ STEP 3: MANAGE YOUR NEEDS

You must keep these above 0:

- Hunger 🍔
- Energy ⚡
- Hygiene 🚿
- Social 💬
- Fun 🎮
- Bladder 🚽

⚠️ Important:
- Hunger at 0 → 💀 instant death
- Other needs at 0 → penalties (hospital, burnout, accidents)

---

## ⏰ STEP 4: RESPECT TIME

- Every action consumes time
- Travel also consumes time
- Needs constantly decrease

🌙 At night:
- Energy drains faster
- Midnight warnings will appear

---

## 💼 STEP 5: EARN INCOME

Get a job early:

- 👨‍🍳 Chef
- 🩺 Doctor
- 🏦 Bank Teller
- 📱 Influencer

💡 Tip:
- Work = stable income
- But overworking = needs collapse ⚠️

---

## 🏠 STEP 6: BUY A HOUSE (PRIORITY)

Your first major goal should be:

👉 🏠 **Buy a house**

Why?
- Unlocks essential survival actions
- Stabilises your gameplay

---

## 📈 Recommended Progression

1. Get stable job 💼
2. Buy house 🏠
3. Buy car 🚗
4. Expand (hotel / pets) 🐾

---

## 💳 STEP 7: USE LOANS CAREFULLY

Loans are NOT free money.

✔ Use only when needed
✔ Always have a repayment plan

⚠️ Ignoring loans = serious consequences

---

## 🎰 STEP 8: CASINO (OPTIONAL RISK)

🚫 Child Sims cannot enter

Available actions:
- 🎰 Slot Machine
- 🃏 Blackjack (21)
- 🚽 Use Toilet
- 🍿 Eat Snack

---

### 🎰 Slot Machine (Quick Guide)

- 777 → **25x payout**
- Other triple → **10x**
- Pair → **2x**
- No match → lose

---

### 🃏 Blackjack (Quick Guide)

- Start with 2 cards
- Choose: Hit / Stand / Double

💡 Rules:
- Double = 1 extra card + auto stand
- 5-card Charlie (≤21) = instant win 🎉
- Natural 21 = 3x
- Normal win = 2x
- Double win = 4x

---

## 🛡 SAFE START STRATEGY

If you’re new, follow this:

1. 👀 Check status every cycle
2. 💼 Work, but keep needs stable
3. 🏠 Save for house
4. ❌ Avoid over-expansion early
5. 🎰 Treat casino as high risk

---

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid these:

- Ignoring time ⏰
- Letting needs stay critical too long ❤️
- Taking loans without a plan 💳
- Buying too many things too early 🏡
- Treating gambling as income 🎰

---

This game rewards:
- planning 🧠
- discipline ⚖️
- timing ⏰
