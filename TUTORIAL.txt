## 🚀 1. Getting Started

When you launch the game, you’ll see:

* **New Game** → Start fresh
* **Continue Game** → Load your save
* **Quit Game** → Exit

👉 First time?
**Pick "New Game" and let the chaos begin.**

---

## 🧍 2. Creating Your First Sim

Go to:

> **Create New Sims**

Choose your Sim type:

* 👶 **Child** → Lower income, slower progression
* 🧑 **Adult** → Balanced gameplay (recommended)
* 👴 **Elder** → Unique bonuses, different needs

Then:

1. Enter a name
2. Select your Sim
3. Enter the Main Menu

💡 **Tip:**
Multiple Sims = shared resources + more strategy (but also more chaos).

---

## 🧠 3. Understanding the Main Menu

**1) View Status**

**2) Travel**

Do things like:

**4) Job Management**
Get rich (or try to)

---

**5) Assets**
Buy/sell car or house

---

**6) Pass Time (+1 hour)**

## ⚠️ 4. Managing Needs (THIS KILLS YOU)

Your Sim has needs:

* 🍔 Hunger
* ⚡ Energy
* 🚿 Hygiene
* 🚽 Bladder
* 🙂 Mood

### Status Colors:

| Level    | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| 🟢 ≥60   | Safe    |
| 🟡 21–59 | Warning |
| 🔴 ≤20   | DANGER  |

### 💀 If ANY need hits 0:

**Your Sim dies instantly. Game Over.**

---

### 🧩 How to fix needs:

| Need    | Solution     |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Hunger  | Restaurant   |
| Energy  | Sleep (Home) |
| Hygiene | Shower       |
| Bladder | Toilet       |
| Mood    | Park         |

---

### 😴 Sleep System

* 20:00 → “You should sleep soon…”
* 21:00 → “SERIOUSLY go sleep”
* 22:00 → 💀 *You collapse*

👉 Sleep = next day (08:00)

## 🏠 6. Assets (Car & House)

Welcome to debt simulator.

---

### 🚗 Car

* Cost: $2000
* Down payment: $400
* Sell value: 60%

**Benefit:**

* Cheaper travel (less energy + hunger)

---

### 🏡 House

* Cost: $5000
* Down payment: $1500
* Sell value: 90%

**Benefit:**

* You can SLEEP (very important 💀)
* Better need recovery

---

### 🧠 Priority:

👉 **House > Car**

---

## 🏦 7. Banking & Loans

### You have:

* 💵 Simcoin (cash)
* 🏦 Bank account

---

### Actions:

* Deposit
* Withdraw
* Take loan
* Repay loan

---

### 💳 Loan Rules:

* Max: $5000
* Used for assets

---

### ⚠️ Consequences:

#### After 60 days:

* You may lose car or house (repossession)

#### After 80 days:

* If money < loan → 💀 YOU DIE

---

💡 **Golden rule:**
Don’t take loans you can’t repay.

---
